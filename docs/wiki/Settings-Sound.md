# Sound settings

Volume levels in the game client.

**5 settings** in the **Sound** category of the Client tab. [All categories](Settings-Reference) · [How to read this page](Settings-Reference#how-to-read-these-pages)

## Volume

### Sound effects volume (SoundVolume)

`Option.ini › [Audio] › SoundVolume` · slider

- **Default:** `0.6`
- **Allowed:** 0 – 1

### Music volume (MusicVolume)

`Option.ini › [Audio] › MusicVolume` · slider

- **Default:** `1`
- **Allowed:** 0 – 1

### Voice volume (WavVoiceVolume)

`Option.ini › [Audio] › WavVoiceVolume` · slider

- **Default:** `0.6`
- **Allowed:** 0 – 1

### NPC voice volume (OggVoiceVolume)

`Option.ini › [Audio] › OggVoiceVolume` · slider

- **Default:** `0.6`
- **Allowed:** 0 – 1

### Mute all sound (AudioMuteOn)

`Option.ini › [Audio] › AudioMuteOn` · on/off

- **Default:** Off
- **Allowed:** On or Off
